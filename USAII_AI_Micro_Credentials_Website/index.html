FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a technical background?

No. The micro credentials are designed for learners without a technical background. No coding is required.

How long does a course take?

Each course takes about 10 hours to complete. You can work through the self paced lessons on your own schedule.

Are the courses self paced?

Yes. All courses are online and self paced. There are no live classes.

Do I have to take the courses in order?

No. Each course stands on its own, so you can begin with the skill that is most relevant to you.

What tools do I need?

You need a web browser and a general purpose AI assistant. Some courses also require a no code agent building tool or an assistant builder that supports document uploads.

Can I apply the learning to my work?

Yes. You can apply the methods to a relevant task. Do not include names, client data, confidential information, or other sensitive material.

What do I receive when I complete a course?

You receive a USAII® Certificate of Completion after meeting the course requirements.

Is the Certificate of Completion the same as a USAII® certification?

No. The Certificate of Completion recognizes completion of an applied micro credential. It is separate from USAII®’s proctored flagship AI certifications.

Are course videos accessible?

Course videos include transcripts.
